## 1. Vision

**A world where every child who needs speech therapy gets to practise every day, with a calm guide that knows them, and a therapist who still owns their care.**

Speech and language is the substrate of childhood — of friendship, learning, identity, and confidence. Today, that substrate is rationed: by waiting lists, by clinic hours, by postcode, by family time. Wulo's long-term vision is to remove practice as the rate-limiting step in paediatric speech therapy without ever removing the therapist from the loop.

In ten years, Wulo aspires to be the operating layer for supervised speech practice across the UK and other publicly funded health and education systems — the place where therapy continues between sessions, where children build fluency through play, and where therapists keep the clinical authorship and the clinical authority.

---

## 2. Mission

**Help speech and language therapists extend their care beyond the session, using AI that listens carefully, scores honestly, and never decides clinically on its own.**

Concretely, that means:

- Giving every child on a therapist's caseload a structured, child-friendly way to practise between appointments.
- Giving every therapist a faster, calmer way to see what happened in that practice and decide what to do next.
- Giving every parent a clear, non-clinical view of their child's effort and progress.
- Giving every clinic, school, and commissioner the evidence they need to fund the practice layer at scale.

---

## 3. The Problem We Solve

The UK paediatric speech and language system is structurally under-resourced and is unlikely to be fixed by hiring alone:

| Signal | Data |
|---|---|
| Children struggling with speech & language in the UK | ~2 million |
| Children on NHS SALT waiting lists (Nov 2024) | 65,114 |
| Waiting more than 12 weeks | 45.6% |
| SLT vacancy rate across UK | 21% (RCSLT, 2023) |
| Primary teachers reporting post-COVID language delays | 67% |

Inside a therapist's week, the binding constraint is not knowledge or empathy — it is **time between sessions**. The same problem repeats in clinic after clinic:

- Children practise inconsistently or not at all between weekly appointments.
- Parents want to help but cannot judge sounds, retries, or progress reliably.
- Therapists lose hours every week to manual review, note-writing, and planning that never reaches the child.
- Schools and waiting-list cohorts get nothing structured at all.

Wulo addresses this single, sharp gap: **the practice layer between sessions**. Everything else in the product — memory, recommendations, planning, dashboards — exists to make that layer trustworthy enough for a clinician to rely on.

|---|---|---|

### Why this is unique (UK)

No current UK competitor combines all three of:

1. **Real-time AI voice interaction** with pronunciation scoring (not static drills, not text guidance).
2. **Therapist-directed exercise authoring and review** (not a consumer app, not a marketplace).
3. **Structured continuity-of-care surface** — memory, recommendations, planning — that fits inside the therapist's existing workflow.

Mable Therapy scales live therapy linearly. SuperPenguin guides caregivers without real-time feedback. BetterSpeech is a US insurance-driven marketplace. Speech Blubs is a consumer app with no clinical workflow. Wulo is the only therapist-supervised practice platform built for the UK SEN system. ## 5. Target Users (Who Wulo Is For)

### Primary beachhead — Independent paediatric SLTs and small clinics
HCPC-registered SLTs in private practice or clinics of 1–10 therapists. They can decide quickly, give direct workflow feedback, and convert without procurement. This is where every product decision is currently weighted.

### Secondary — SEND schools, special schools, MATs
SENCOs, inclusion leads, and speech & language leads supporting children on waiting lists or between SLT sessions. Activated only after therapist validation and at least one credible workflow proof point.

### Later — Local authorities and NHS community providers
Commissioning leads, AHP leads, ICB children's therapy managers running waiting-list cohorts. Enabled when governance, evidence, and pilot operations are mature enough to clear procurement.

### End users (always therapist-supervised)
- **Children with SEN, primarily 5–11**, with articulation, phonology, DLD, early language, and related needs.
- **Parents and caregivers** as supervised supporters, never as clinical decision-makers.

## 6. What Wulo Is — and Is Not

### Wulo IS
- A **therapist-supervised practice and review platform** for paediatric speech therapy.
- A way for clinicians to **author exercises, assign practice, and review evidence** with less manual effort.
- A **calm, child-friendly AI practice buddy** that runs short, structured voice exercises.
- A **continuity-of-care layer**: governed child memory, explainable recommendations, Copilot-backed next-session planning.
- **UK-hosted, GDPR-aligned, safeguarding-first** by design.

### Wulo IS NOT
- Not a **diagnostic or assessment tool**.
- Not a **replacement for a qualified SLT**.
- Not an **unsupervised consumer app** for children.
- Not a **teletherapy marketplace**.
- Not an **autonomous AI clinician** — every clinically meaningful output is reviewable, editable, and gated by the therapist.

This boundary is a product principle, not just messaging. It governs roadmap decisions and is the reason features like child memory and planning are explicitly governed (approval-gated) rather than auto-applied.

## 7. Operating Principles

These are the rules we hold the product to, especially when AI capabilities tempt shortcuts.

1. **Therapist authorship is sacred.** Targets, exercises, and plans are authored or approved by the therapist. AI proposes; clinicians dispose.
2. **Practice, not diagnosis.** Wulo is a practice and review tool. It does not produce diagnostic claims.
3. **Explainable by default.** Recommendations and memory items must show their evidence. No black-box clinical outputs.
4. **Calm over clever.** Child-facing UX is warm, slow, and supportive. Never gamified into noise.
5. **Privacy is a feature.** UK hosting, consent gating, audit logs, export and deletion are first-class, not bolt-ons.
6. **Therapist time is the scarce resource.** Every feature is judged on whether it gives clinicians visible time back.
7. **Evidence over hype.** Outcomes data, adherence, and review-time metrics are the language we sell in — not AI buzzwords.
8. **Human-in-the-loop is the moat.** Governed memory, approvals, and review are what make Wulo trustworthy in a regulated context.

## 8. Product Pillars

The current shipped product (live at sen.wulo.ai) maps to four pillars:

1. **Guided Child Practice** — avatar-led real-time voice sessions; therapist-authored and built-in exercises; pronunciation scoring; supportive retries.
2. **Therapist Workspace** — authenticated home, dashboard, session review, charts, exercise authoring, workspace switching, invitations, privacy controls.
3. **Governed Continuity of Care** — proposed child memory items the therapist approves or rejects; explainable next-exercise recommendations; Copilot-backed next-session planning.
4. **Trustworthy Infrastructure** — Azure Container Apps, Azure AI Foundry, Azure Speech, Postgres-with-RLS persistence, Easy Auth (Google + Microsoft), audit and consent flows, UK hosting.

## 10. Business Model (Summary)

SaaS subscription, segmented by buyer:

- **Therapist** — Starter (free, 3 children), Pro (£49/mo), Clinic (£39/mo per seat for 3+).
- **School** — Pilot (£1,500/term, up to 30 children), Full (£4,000/yr, up to 60 children).
- **Local Authority / NHS** — Cohort-based pilots (£10K–£25K/yr) and borough-wide custom contracts.

Pricing philosophy: low enough for a single therapist to say yes without procurement; never per-child for therapists (perverse incentives). Full GTM detail in [WULO-GTM-PLAN.md](WULO-GTM-PLAN.md).
