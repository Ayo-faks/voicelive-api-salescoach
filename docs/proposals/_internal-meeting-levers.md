# Internal — Meeting levers (not for sending)

These are the numbers behind the IP options in the Limi and Unlocking Language proposals. The proposals deliberately leave them out so the partner has something to negotiate. Open with these in the meeting; do not commit in writing until the pilot agreement.

## Exercise authoring — three options

### (a) Keep private
- Default. No negotiation needed.
- Their IP, their workspace, never leaves.

### (b) Licence to Wulo for credit (discount-for-credit)
- **Open at:** £250 / ₦400,000 off the next annual renewal per exercise that ships into the public library.
- **Cap:** 50% of the annual licence. Above the cap, any further contributions roll into option (c).
- **Eligibility:** exercise must be reviewed by Wulo's clinical lead and pass a quality bar before it counts toward the discount.
- **Why this works for us:** caps the discount exposure, gives them a tangible reason to author quality content, content stays attributed.

### (c) Revenue share
- **Open at:** 20% of the per-active-child line item that the exercise appears in, paid quarterly.
- **Range to negotiate to:** 15–25%. 15% if they want broader distribution and lower friction; 25% only if the content is unusually differentiated (e.g. a Nigerian English articulation library Limi authors, or an EAL/multilingual library UL authors).
- **Floor:** £/₦ minimum per quarter so they don't get sub-£50 cheques. Suggest £100/₦150,000 minimum.
- **Termination:** if they stop being a customer, royalty continues for the longer of (i) 24 months or (ii) the lifetime of the active children using those exercises at the moment of termination. Do not commit longer than that — open-ended royalties are a balance-sheet risk.

## Pilot pricing — the floor

- **UL implementation fee:** £1,250. Floor is £750 — only drop if they bring a second clinic into Phase 2 within the pilot window.
- **Limi implementation fee:** ₦450,000. Floor is ₦300,000 — only drop if they commit to introducing Wulo to one named peer hospital (e.g. NHA, Reddington, Nizamiye) by Week 12.
- **UL annual licence:** £12k–£24k. Indicative range. Final pricing post-pilot, based on therapist count + commissioned contracts in scope.
- **Limi annual licence:** ₦4.5M–₦7.5M. Same logic.

## Calibration / design-partner credit (new — both proposals)

The proposals now explicitly separate the implementation fee from the calibration/design-partner contribution, and convert that contribution into a Year-1 discount.

- **Per-entry credit values used in the proposal worked examples:**
  - **UL:** ~£17 per accepted contribution (override / accent calibration entry / accepted workflow change). 120 entries → ~£2,000.
  - **Limi:** ~₦5,000 per accepted calibration entry. 150 entries → ~₦750,000.
- **Cap:** 50% of Year-1 annual licence. Hard cap. Do not lift in the meeting — above the cap, push them toward the revenue-share path on exercise authoring instead.
- **What counts as a "contribution":** clinician override, accent calibration entry, or accepted workflow change. *Accepted* is the keyword — Wulo clinical lead reviews; not every override auto-qualifies. Keep this gate; it stops the credit becoming a click-farm.
- **If they walk after the pilot:** credit is acknowledged in the Evidence Pack and case study but has no cash value. Do not offer cash-out. Ever.
- **Stacking with the exercise-authoring options:** credit applies to *calibration / workflow* contributions only. Exercise authoring is the separate three-option IP framework (private / licence-for-credit / revenue share). They can stack — but the combined discount on Year-1 is still capped at 50% of the annual licence.

## Things to never give away in the meeting

- Free pilot. Walk before agreeing to a £0 / ₦0 implementation fee. The fee is what makes them prioritise it internally.
- Perpetual royalty after they leave. Cap at 24 months or active-cohort lifetime.
- Exclusivity of any kind in the design-partner phase. They are first, not only.
- Commitment to white-label / co-brand inside their commissioned tenders before Phase 3 outcomes are in.

## Levers we *can* give

- One extra mid-pilot co-design session.
- A named clinical credit on the public Wulo case study and on the Evidence Pack cover.
- First-mover discount on the Year-2 licence (10–15%) if they sign within 30 days of pilot close.
- Joint conference submission (RCSLT for UL; West African paediatric / ENT body for Limi).
