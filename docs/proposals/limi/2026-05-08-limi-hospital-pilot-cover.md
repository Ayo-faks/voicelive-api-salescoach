### The problem

Paediatric speech and post‑ENT outcomes are limited by the 167 hours a week the child is at home. Prescribed practice is inconsistently completed, parents have no structured way to report on it, and the next consultation starts with reconstruction instead of progress.

### The proposal

A 12‑week clinical pilot at The Limi Children's Hospital. 20–30 paediatric patients from Limi's existing caseload, run by a named Limi Clinical Lead, against pre‑agreed adherence and outcome thresholds.

### How it works

1. **Prescribe.** The Limi clinician assigns target sounds, words, or language goals from a web dashboard.
2. **Practise.** The child completes 3–5 minute guided sessions at home on the family's own phone or tablet.
3. **Review.** Before the next visit, the clinician opens a one‑screen summary of accuracy and adherence.

Wulo does not diagnose, does not replace the clinician, and is not marketed to families independently.

### Why it fits Limi

- **Compliance.** Converts prescribed practice into evidenced practice, and surfaces non‑adherence early.
- **Post‑surgical pathway.** Gives ENT and paediatrics a structured rehabilitation route after adenoidectomy, grommets, and cleft repair.
- **Low operational burden.** No device procurement, no IT integration. ~60 minutes/week from one Clinical Lead.

### Success criteria (Evidence Pack)

| Metric | Baseline method | Week‑12 threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Adherence** | 8‑week retrospective audit of case notes for the cohort. | ≥ 60% of children complete 2+ Wulo sessions/week for 8 of 12 weeks. |
| **Clinic efficiency** | Timed reconstruction segment, 10 consultations at Week 1. | ≥ 30% reduction at Week 12. |
| **Parent experience** | Pre‑pilot survey of enrolled families (NPS + 3 structured questions). | Post‑pilot NPS ≥ +30. |
| **Speech progression** | Standardised single‑word probe per child by the Clinical Lead. | ≥ 50% of cohort show clinically meaningful improvement. |

**Go / no‑go.** The pilot is successful if at least 3 of 4 thresholds are met **and adherence is one of them**.

### Data and governance (headline only; full detail in the proposal)

- **Compliance.** Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023); DPIA signed before any patient is enrolled.
- **Controller / processor.** Limi is Data Controller; Neuter Labs Ltd is Data Processor under a signed DPA.
- **Hosting.** Microsoft Azure, region agreed with Limi at pilot start. No data sold or shared with third parties.
- **Audio.** Voice is processed in‑memory for pronunciation scoring and discarded by default; no persistent audio is stored unless Limi explicitly opts in to bounded clip retention for case review.
- **Model training.** Patient voice data is not used to train Wulo's models during the pilot.
- **IP.** Exercises authored by Limi clinicians remain Limi's property.
