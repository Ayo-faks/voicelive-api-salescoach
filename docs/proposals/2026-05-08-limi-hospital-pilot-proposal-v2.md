## Executive summary

The Limi Hospital has built 41 years of trust by being deliberate about clinical quality. The single biggest limit on paediatric speech outcomes is not what happens inside the consultation — it is what happens in the **167 hours a week the child is not in clinic**. This is the carryover gap.

**Wulo** is a clinician‑supervised digital practice tool that closes that gap. The Limi clinician prescribes the practice; the child completes short guided sessions at home; the clinician returns to a structured review before the next visit. Wulo is not a therapist and does not pretend to be one — it is a *digital prescription for practice*, with the clinician fully in control.

We are proposing a **12‑week clinical pilot** at Limi with one purpose: produce hard adherence and outcome evidence for Limi's paediatric and ENT services. On   partner with first‑mover positioning in AI‑augmented paediatric rehabilitation.

## Why this matters to Limi specifically

- **Patient compliance is the core problem.** Every paediatrician, ENT surgeon, and therapist at Limi knows the same frustration: the plan is right, the family means well, the homework doesn't get done. Wulo is, before anything else, a compliance tool.
- **ENT post‑surgical rehabilitation.** In Nigeria, paediatric speech difficulty is frequently caught downstream of hearing loss, adenoidectomy, tonsillectomy, or cleft repair. Wulo is a natural **post‑surgical rehabilitation pathway** that converts a one‑off surgical episode into a continuous recovery relationship — and a recurring revenue line — for the ENT and paediatrics service.
- **Family‑centred mandate.** Limi's positioning as a multispecialty group *for the family* aligns directly with Wulo's design: the clinician leads, the parent has a clear supporting role, and the child practises in a calm, child‑friendly format.
- **First‑mover prestige.** No hospital in West Africa has yet deployed clinician‑supervised AI speech rehabilitation. We want Limi to set that standard.

## What Wulo is (and isn't)

Wulo is a **digital prescription for practice**. One loop:

1. **The clinician sets the path.** The Limi therapist or paediatrician selects target phonemes, words, or language goals — or authors custom exercises for a specific child.
2. **The child practises.** Short 3–5 minute guided sessions with a calm AI buddy. Gentle prompts, retries, word‑level pronunciation feedback.
3. **The parent has a clear role.** Plain‑English visibility into what was practised and how it went. No clinical jargon, no guesswork.
4. **The clinician reviews structured data, not recordings.** Before the next visit, the clinician opens a one‑screen summary — e.g. *"Child struggled with /s/ blends in initial position; strong on final /k/"* — and starts the consultation with context, not catch‑up.

**Wulo is not** a diagnostic tool, a replacement for a clinician, or an unsupervised consumer app. It is a clinician‑led practice and review layer.

## Exercise authoring and IP

Any exercises Limi clinicians author in Wulo remain Limi's intellectual property. By default they are private to Limi's workspace. If Limi chooses to share specific exercises into Wulo's wider library, three options are available:

- **Keep private** — they stay Limi's alone.
- **Licence to Wulo for credit** — Wulo can use them, attributed to Limi as the clinical author, in exchange for a discount on Limi's annual licence.
- **Revenue share** — Wulo can include them in paid tiers and pays Limi a royalty per active child using them.

Terms agreed in writing before any exercise leaves Limi's workspace. Limi is never asked to give clinical IP away for free.

## Pilot framework

The pilot is scoped by **patient cohort**.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Setting** | The Limi Children's Hospital |
| **Cohort** | 20–30 children, ages 4–12, identified by Limi clinicians |
| **Indications** | Articulation delay, post‑ENT rehabilitation (post‑adenoidectomy, post‑grommet, post‑cleft), language carryover, paediatric follow‑up |
| **Primary leads** | 1 Clinical Lead (SLT, paediatrician, or ENT) + 1 Admin Liaison |
| **Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2** | Onboarding, clinician training, calibration baseline, family enrolment |
| **Phase 2 — Weeks 3–10** | Active practice, weekly clinician review, mid‑pilot calibration update |
| **Phase 3 — Weeks 11–12** | Outcome measurement, family survey, evidence pack delivery |

---

## Clinical governance and data security

- **Regulatory.** Fully aligned with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023)**. A short DPIA is shared and signed before any patient is enrolled.
- **Infrastructure.** Microsoft Azure, enterprise‑grade encryption in transit and at rest.
- **Audio handling.** No audio recordings are permanently retained. Speech is processed into structured data points (phoneme accuracy, retry count, engagement). Child identity is protected.
- **Consent.** Aligned to Limi's existing paediatric consent process. Parents can withdraw and request data deletion at any time.

## Success metrics — the Evidence Pack

The pilot is judged against **Limi's own current numbers**, not against a generic benchmark. In Week 1, the Clinical Lead and the Wulo team jointly record Limi's existing baseline for each metric (using clinician estimates and recent case notes where direct measurement isn't already in place). At Week 12, the Evidence Pack reports the change against that baseline.

| # | Metric | Baseline captured at Week 1 | Result reported at Week 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Adherence rate** | % of children currently completing prescribed home practice (clinician‑estimated) | % of cohort completing ≥2 Wulo sessions/week |
| 2 | **Quality of consultation** *(transition from manual catch‑up to data‑driven consultation)* | Minutes the clinician currently spends at the start of a session reconstructing what happened at home, and what proportion of the consultation is catch‑up vs. forward progress | Minutes spent reviewing the structured Wulo summary, and clinician‑rated proportion of the next consultation spent on forward clinical decisions rather than reconstruction |
| 3 | **Parental experience** | Limi's current paediatric satisfaction signal (existing survey or NPS, if available) | Post‑pilot NPS from enrolled families |
| 4 | **Speech progression** | Clinician‑rated baseline phoneme accuracy per enrolled child | Quantitative phoneme accuracy trajectory over 12 weeks, clinician‑validated |

The pilot is considered successful if Wulo shows a meaningful positive movement against Limi's baseline on at least three of the four metrics, and the Clinical Lead would recommend continuing.

The Evidence Pack is Limi's, regardless of what Limi decides next. It can be used internally, in board reporting, or in publication.

## Beyond the pilot

Three options, Limi's choice:

- **Stop.** Limi keeps the Evidence Pack. No fees, no lock‑in.
- **Extend** the cohort at the Children's Hospital to 60–100 active children for a further 12 weeks on the same terms, and bring in adjacent Limi services (ENT outpatient, paediatric follow‑up clinics).
- **Adopt** under an annual licence covering the Children's Hospital and any Limi service that sees paediatric patients. At adoption, Wulo configures a **Limi‑branded portal** as standard. **Indicative annual licensing: ₦4.5M – ₦7.5M**, subject to number of clinicians, active children, and pilot data.
