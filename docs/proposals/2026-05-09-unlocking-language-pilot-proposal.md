## Executive summary

Unlocking Language has spent over a decade solving the *access* side of paediatric speech and language therapy in the UK — no waiting lists, appointments within 48 hours, clinics in Canary Wharf and Romford, and commissioned services across schools, NHS Trusts, and ICBs. You have already removed the slowest part of the patient journey.

The remaining bottleneck is the same one every SLT practice in the country lives with: **what happens in the 167 hours a week the child is not in front of a therapist.** Carryover is fragile. Parents mean well. Homework gets done sometimes. Therapists return to a session not knowing what really happened at home, and the first ten minutes get spent on reconstruction rather than progress.

**Wulo** is a clinician‑supervised practice and review layer designed to close exactly that gap. The Unlocking Language therapist sets the targets; the child practises in short, child‑friendly guided sessions between visits; the therapist returns to a structured one‑screen review. Wulo is not a therapist and does not pretend to be one — it is *therapist‑directed practice*, with the SLT fully in control.

We are proposing a **12‑week design‑partner pilot** with 2–3 of your therapists and 15–25 children from your existing caseload. The pilot is structured so Unlocking Language helps shape the therapist workflow itself — you are not a customer, you are a co‑designer. On success, there is a clear path to practice‑wide deployment and to embedding Wulo inside your commissioned school and ICB contracts as a differentiator.

## What Wulo is (and isn't)

Wulo is a **therapist‑directed practice layer between sessions.** One loop:

1. **The therapist sets the path.** The Unlocking Language SLT selects target phonemes, words, or language goals — or authors custom exercises for a specific child.
2. **The child practises.** Short 3–5 minute guided sessions with a calm AI buddy. Gentle prompts, retries, word‑level pronunciation feedback.
3. **The parent has a clear role.** Plain‑English visibility into what was practised and how it went. No clinical jargon, no guesswork.
4. **The therapist reviews structured data, not recordings.** Before the next session, the SLT opens a one‑screen summary — e.g. *"Child struggled with /s/ blends in initial position; strong on final /k/"* — and starts the appointment with context, not catch‑up.

**Wulo is not** a diagnostic tool, a replacement for an SLT, an unsupervised consumer app, or an "AI therapist." It is therapist‑led practice and review.

**Built on Microsoft Azure** (Azure AI Foundry, Azure Speech, Azure Container Apps). Live today at `sen.wulo.ai`. UK‑hosted, UK GDPR‑aligned.

## The design-partner frame

Unlocking Language is not the right partner for a polished, take‑it‑or‑leave‑it product. You are the right partner to **shape** the therapist workflow before it goes to broader UK practices.

This pilot is structured so your therapists materially influence the product:

- **Therapist workflow override.** Your SLTs can adjust the AI's strictness per target sound, per child, and flag specific phonemes where the AI's judgement should be ignored or relaxed. Their overrides are captured and improve the model.
- **Exercise authoring — your IP, your choice.** Any exercises your therapists author in Wulo remain Unlocking Language's intellectual property. By default they are private to your workspace. If you choose to share specific exercises into Wulo's wider library, you have three options: (a) **keep private** — they stay yours alone; (b) **license to Wulo for credit** — Wulo can use them, attributed to Unlocking Language as the clinical author, in exchange for a discount on your annual licence; (c) **revenue share** — Wulo can include them in paid tiers and pays Unlocking Language a royalty per active child using them. Terms agreed in writing before any exercise leaves your workspace. You are never asked to give clinical IP away for free.
- **Workflow co‑design sessions.** Two 60‑minute sessions during the pilot where your senior therapists tell us what's missing in the dashboard, planner, and review screens. Their changes ship.
- **Co‑published case material.** With your consent, the pilot becomes the basis of a joint case study and a podcast episode for *your* podcast — content that strengthens both brands.

You are not a test site. You are the senior clinical voice that defines how this technology fits a serious UK SLT practice.

## Clinical governance and data security

- **Regulatory.** Fully aligned with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. A short DPIA is shared and signed before any child is enrolled.
- **Infrastructure.** Microsoft Azure UK regions, enterprise‑grade encryption in transit and at rest.
- **Audio handling.** No audio recordings are permanently retained. Speech is processed into structured data points (phoneme accuracy, retry count, engagement). Child identity is protected.
- **Consent.** Aligned to Unlocking Language's existing parental consent process. Parents can withdraw and request data deletion at any time.
- **Safeguarding.** RCSLT and HCPC posture respected — Wulo does not give clinical advice to families, does not screen, does not assess. It delivers practice that the SLT has prescribed.
- **Connectivity.** Lightweight web app, no native install. Works on standard home broadband and UK mobile networks. Low‑bandwidth fallback for poor signal.

## Success metrics — the Evidence Pack

The pilot is judged against **Unlocking Language's own current numbers**, not against a generic benchmark. In Week 1, the lead SLT and the Wulo team jointly record your existing baseline for each metric (using clinician estimates and recent case notes where direct measurement isn't already in place). At Week 12, the Evidence Pack reports the change against that baseline.

| # | Metric | Baseline captured at Week 1 | Result reported at Week 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Adherence rate** | % of children currently completing prescribed home practice (clinician‑estimated) | % of cohort completing ≥2 Wulo sessions/week |
| 2 | **Quality of consultation** *(transition from manual catch‑up to data‑driven review)* | Minutes the SLT currently spends at the start of a session reconstructing what happened at home, and the proportion of the session spent on catch‑up vs. forward progress | Minutes spent reviewing the structured Wulo summary, and SLT‑rated proportion of the next session spent on forward clinical decisions rather than reconstruction |
| 3 | **Parental experience** | Unlocking Language's current parent satisfaction signal (existing survey or NPS, if available) | Post‑pilot NPS from enrolled families |
| 4 | **Speech progression** | SLT‑rated baseline phoneme/target accuracy per enrolled child | Quantitative trajectory over 12 weeks, SLT‑validated |

The pilot is considered successful if Wulo shows a meaningful positive movement against your baseline on at least three of the four metrics, and the lead SLT would recommend continuing.

The Evidence Pack is yours, regardless of what you decide next — usable internally, in commissioned tenders, in EHCP reviews, on your website, or in publication.
